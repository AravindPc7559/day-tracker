Delete Your CaptureDay Account

Last updated: June 29, 2026

CaptureDay lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1: Delete in-app (recommended)

  1. Open the CaptureDay app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all of your data — there is no waiting period.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email codewitharavind7559@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and request account deletion. We will process your request within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (email, linked Google account)
  • All day-tracking entries, logs, categories, and streak/progress data
  • Push notification tokens

What may be retained

  • Subscription/purchase transaction records may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for billing, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes, independent of your CaptureDay account.

Partial data deletion

You don't need to delete your whole account to remove specific data — individual day-tracking entries can be deleted directly within the app without affecting the rest of your account.
