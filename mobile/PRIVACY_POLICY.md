# Privacy Policy for CaptureDay

**Last updated:** June 29, 2026

CaptureDay ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal day-tracking app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address and basic profile info (name, photo) via Firebase Authentication, including Google Sign-In.

### Content You Create
- Text, voice recordings, and photos you submit to log your day's activities ("entries").
- Voice recordings are transcribed to text using a third-party AI service (OpenAI) and are not stored after transcription.
- Photos (e.g. receipts) are analyzed by a third-party AI service (OpenAI) to extract category/summary data, then deleted from our servers immediately after processing.
- The resulting text entries, categories, and streak/progress data are stored in our database (Google Firebase/Firestore) tied to your account.

### Subscription & Purchase Information
- If you subscribe, purchase and entitlement data is processed by RevenueCat and the Google Play Billing system. We do not see or store your payment card details.

### Device Permissions
- **Microphone**: to record voice entries for transcription.
- **Camera / Photo Library**: to capture or select images (e.g. receipts) for logging.
- **Notifications**: to send you reminders related to your tracked activities.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To provide core app functionality: creating, storing, and displaying your day-tracking entries.
- To authenticate you and keep your data private to your account.
- To process subscription purchases and manage access to paid features.
- To send optional reminder notifications.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We share data with the following third parties only as needed to provide the service:
- **Firebase (Google)** — authentication and data storage.
- **OpenAI** — transcribing voice recordings and analyzing images you submit.
- **RevenueCat** — managing subscriptions and purchase validation.
- **Google Play Billing** — processing in-app purchases.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf.

## 4. Data Retention
- Your entries and account data are retained as long as your account is active.
- Voice recordings and uploaded images are not retained after they are processed into text/category data.
- You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see Section 7).

## 5. Data Security
We use industry-standard practices (encrypted connections, Firebase Authentication, server-side validation) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Children's Privacy
CaptureDay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
